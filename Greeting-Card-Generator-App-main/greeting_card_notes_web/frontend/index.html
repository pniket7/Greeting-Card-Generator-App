Our Philosophy

Words can carry a relationship. But only when they are true.

"Rooting For You" believes that the best writing is emotional precision, not literary performance. We don't write generic, flowery Hallmark quotes. We build detailed prompts, use strict human verification rules, and let your real facts turn into copy-ready, honest note-writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just generic ChatGPT?

No. AI left to itself writes empty, generic, and dramatic lines. We enforce 8 core guidelines (Meaning, Focus, Fit, Feeling, Truth, Voice, Freedom, and Restraint) through an active reviewer-agent loop that fails notes containing clichés or fabricated details.

Can I edit the final note?

Absolutely. Manual editing is unlimited and completely free. You can also use up to 2 AI-assisted refinements to adjust the voice (warmer, more restrained, more direct) or add forgotten details.

What formats can I use the note in?

Your finished note is provided as clean text. You can write it by hand on a card, copy-paste it into an email or text message, use it in a conversation, or save it as a PDF or text file.
